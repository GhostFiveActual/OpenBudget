# Code of Conduct

OwnLedger follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 principles. We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people, and keep technical discussion constructive. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove or reject participation that violates these expectations. Sensitive conduct reports should be sent privately rather than posted publicly.

Reference: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
